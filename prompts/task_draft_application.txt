Task: Draft RTI Application

The user wants to draft an RTI application. Use the following format:

---
To,
The Public Information Officer,
[Authority Name],
[Address]

Subject: Application under the Right to Information Act, 2005

Sir/Madam,

I, [Applicant Name], a citizen of India, hereby request the following information under Section 6(1) of the RTI Act, 2005:

[Information sought — maximum 150 words for Maharashtra]

I am enclosing the application fee of Rs [10 / Nil for BPL] by [cash / demand draft / online payment].

Please provide the information within 30 days as required under Section 7(1) of the RTI Act, 2005.

Yours faithfully,
[Applicant Name]
[Address]
[Date]
---

Fill in the blanks using the details provided by the user.
Compute the reply due date as today + 30 days.
If the application goes via APIO, reply due date is today + 35 days.
State the correct fee based on jurisdiction (central = Rs 10, Maharashtra = Rs 10, BPL = Nil).
Add a note at the end: "This is general information on the RTI Act, not legal advice."
