# RTI Mitra — 10 User Questions

These are real questions a citizen using RTI Mitra might ask.
Q6 is a question that the current data cannot answer.

---

**Q1.** What is the RTI application fee in Maharashtra?

**Q2.** Within how many days must the PIO reply to an RTI?

**Q3.** Can I ask for information about my own panchayat in Marathi?

**Q4.** Draft an RTI to Kopargaon Panchayat Samiti asking for the 2026
road-repair work order and payment status, and tell me when I can file
a first appeal if there is no reply.

**Q5.** What is the difference between filing an RTI to a central ministry
online and filing one to a Maharashtra department online?

**Q6.** What did the PIO of Kopargaon municipal council reply to RTIs
filed last month?

> This question cannot be answered — RTI Mitra does not have access to
> live PIO reply records or past RTI case outcomes. The model must say
> "I don't know" for this.

**Q7.** Am I exempt from paying the RTI fee if I am below the poverty line?

**Q8.** What happens if the PIO does not reply within 30 days?

**Q9.** Can I file an RTI through an APIO at my nearest post office, and
does that change the reply deadline?

**Q10.** What information is exempt from RTI disclosure under Section 8,
and does that affect my question about the road-repair work order?
